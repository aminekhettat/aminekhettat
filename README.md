## 🧑‍💻 About Me

I am a **blind engineer** with a deep passion for the elegant intersection of hardware and software. My expertise spans:

- 🔌 **Embedded Systems** — microcontrollers, real-time control, low-level firmware
- 📡 **Signal Processing** — from raw sensor data to meaningful insight
- 🤖 **Automation & Robotics** — making machines move purposefully
- 🎛️ **Motor Control** — BLDC, PMSM, servo — if it spins, I love it

> *"I build software I cannot see, so I make sure everyone else can use it effortlessly."*

Every graphical project I ship is crafted with **accessibility at its core** — full screen-reader compatibility, keyboard-first navigation, and high-contrast design. This is not a feature checkbox; it is my engineering standard.

---

## 🎵 Beyond the Terminal

Music is the other language I speak fluently. I play, learn, and endlessly experiment — which is why several of my projects live at the crossroads of **engineering and music**. Turning a motor into a rhythm machine or a tuner into a blind-friendly instrument? That's just a Tuesday for me.

## 🚀 Projects

### 🔄 [BLDC-Simulator](https://github.com/aminekhettat/BLDC-Simulator)
> **Open-source simulator for BLDC & PMSM motors**

Simulate Brushless DC and Permanent Magnet Synchronous Motors with an advanced feature set. The GUI is built with **full accessibility support**, making motor-control education available to all engineers regardless of visual ability. Perfect for students, researchers, and hobbyists who want to experiment with motor parameters without touching hardware.

`Python` · `Motor Control` · `Signal Processing` · `Accessible GUI`

---

### 🎹 [Back-Office-Player](https://github.com/aminekhettat/Back-Office-Player)
> **Audio player designed for music learning — no sheet music required**

A purpose-built audio player that helps musicians learn and practice pieces without needing to read traditional notation. Whether you are a sighted learner or rely on hearing alone, this player gives you the controls you actually need: slow down, loop, mark sections, and listen your way into mastery.

`Python` · `Audio` · `Music Learning` · `Accessibility`

---

### 🔌 [Easy-LIN](https://github.com/aminekhettat/Easy-LIN)
> **Full-featured LIN bus communication tool with LDF file management**

A no-frills LIN protocol tool for embedded engineers who work with automotive and industrial networks. Handles LDF file parsing, frame scheduling, and diagnostic sessions — everything you need to develop and debug LIN-based systems without the hassle of heavyweight vendor software.

`Python` · `Embedded Systems` · `Automotive` · `LIN Bus` · `LDF`

---

### 🎸 [TuneAccess](https://github.com/aminekhettat/TuneAccess)
> **Musical instrument tuner adapted for visual disability**

A fully accessible chromatic instrument tuner. No visual needle — just clear audio and speech-based pitch feedback so any musician can tune their instrument independently. Built from lived experience: every interaction is designed to be intuitive without sight.

`Python` · `Signal Processing` · `Accessibility` · `Music`

---

### 📊 [SVM-Analyst](https://github.com/aminekhettat/SVM-analyst)
> **Support Vector Machine data analysis toolkit**

A machine learning analysis tool centred around Support Vector Machines. Useful for classification and signal-analysis workflows in engineering contexts — data preprocessing, training, evaluation, all in one place.

`Python` · `Machine Learning` · `SVM` · `Data Analysis`
